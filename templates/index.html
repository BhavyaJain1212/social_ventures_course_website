The Challenge

Why Artisans Need Design Intelligence

Limited Market Visibility

Many artisans don't have access to changing market preferences, trending aesthetics, or modern product design signals.

Repeating Old Patterns

Without trend awareness, skilled artisans continue making traditional products while customer taste moves toward lighter, contemporary designs.

Wasted Resources

Creating products without market validation risks time, material, and effort — resources that artisans can't afford to waste.
